B2B SEO / BERKARA / TURKEY

423 SKUs, connected by a clear search strategy.

For BERKARA, we built an SEO foundation connecting a technical product range, Turkish search demand and real purchasing scenarios.

An industrial catalogue can easily become a collection of pages that buyers never find.

The goal was to create a clear architecture so each product could contribute to visibility and the next enquiry.

About the project

BERKARA supplies industrial wheels to B2B customers in Turkey. Its catalogue covers 423 technical products for different industries and applications.

The client’s challenge

Make a large supplier catalogue easier for procurement teams to navigate, with an SEO structure that helps buyers find the right industrial wheel.

Context

A complex product needs to be found before the first call.

B2B · SEO · Industrial products · Turkey

The supplier had a strong product range, but search engines and new customers needed a clearer way to navigate it. The catalogue required a consistent structure.

We organised demand by category, specification and application, then defined rules for product pages, metadata, URLs, alt text and pages without commercial demand. This makes SEO part of the sales process.

Every B2B product page should answer a buyer’s question: “Will this solve my problem?”
