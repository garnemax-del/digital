MEDICAL TOURISM / GLOBALMEDIK

Building a marketing system that attracts relevant enquiries.

Marketing for an international medical company: strategy, performance advertising, YouTube and content in a market where trust is essential.

Medical tourism involves a long decision process. Patients need to understand whom they can trust with their health and money.

We worked across the decision-making process, connecting the channels involved.

About the project

GLOBALMEDIK is a medical tourism service in Turkey, supporting international patients from choosing a clinic and doctor through treatment and their return home.

The client’s challenge

Help international patients understand the services, build trust in the team and request a consultation through search, the website and content.

Since January 2023 · ongoing partnership

2023 →a long-term team partnership
Googlea channel for high-intent demand
YouTubean organic channel for building trust
Context

In medical marketing, a lead starts a conversation.

Medical tourism · International audiences · Ongoing partnership

GLOBALMEDIK had a strong offering. Its communication needed to answer patient questions, respect advertising restrictions and attract enquiries its coordinators could meaningfully assist with.

We connected paid demand, expert content and a clear path from first contact to enquiry, avoiding aggressive advertising and unsupported promises.

Medical marketing depends on being trusted.
Digital Clever’s approach
What we built

Building the foundations of trust.

01

Mapping the patient’s decisions

We identified questions and concerns at each stage, from researching procedures to choosing a clinic and speaking with a coordinator.

02

Connecting paid channels

We connected Meta and Google, distinguished high-intent demand from awareness activity and developed measurement and ongoing optimisation.

03

Adding expert content

YouTube and practical content answered complex questions before an enquiry and helped build trust organically.

04

Working on enquiry quality

We considered each lead’s relevance to the business, including the request, readiness and fit with the service.

Outcome

Developing the quality of the conversation.

Connected channels help marketing support the sales process consistently.
