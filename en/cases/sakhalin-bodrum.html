HOSPITALITY / PERFORMANCE

Sakhalin Bodrum: more enquiries on the same budget

A seasonal premium restaurant in Bodrum, competing with restaurants, beach clubs and music venues for summer visitors. We connected always-on booking campaigns, event promotion and Instagram growth with monthly performance reviews.

The client’s challenge

Generate enquiries during a limited season and allocate spend between general bookings, Tuna Show and live music evenings. The team needed to identify which offers prompted people to message the restaurant.

What we did

01

Always-on bookings

General Booking became the main source of enquiries. In August, it generated 141 enquiries at $6.09 each, accounting for approximately 86% of the month’s total.

02

Events as distinct offers

Tuna Show and live music evenings had separate campaigns, allowing us to compare cost and message conversion by format.

03

Separate audience growth and performance

Instagram Traffic focused on audience growth. Its spend was excluded when calculating cost per enquiry for performance campaigns.

04

Monthly decisions informed by data

We compared impressions, clicks, enquiries and cost per result. The July report showed higher enquiry costs for live music evenings; in August, the always-on booking campaign generated most enquiries.

Project outcome

August delivered 28 more enquiries than July. Total advertising spend decreased by 0.8%, while cost per enquiry for performance campaigns fell by approximately 18.3%. These are campaign results for specific months: enquiries are not confirmed bookings or visits.
