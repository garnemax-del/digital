BRAND IDENTITY / HOSPITALITY LAUNCH / MAZI

A restaurant starts building its reputation before the first reservation.

For MAZI, we developed the brand foundations: positioning, original characters, content and a clear path from search to the table.

In Istanbul’s busy tourist centre, the restaurant needed to stand out among dozens of alternatives.

Its cuisine, atmosphere and service needed to be clear in English and Turkish before a guest’s first visit.

About the project

MAZI is an Ottoman palace cuisine restaurant in Istanbul’s historic centre. It reinterprets palace recipes with contemporary presentation for tourists and international guests.

The client’s challenge

Create a recognisable restaurant brand and attract its first guests through content, Google and reviews.

Context

A strong restaurant brand gives guests confidence in their choice.

Brand identity · hospitality · launch · reputation

Guests often choose quickly, using maps, reviews, photographs and a few seconds on a page. MAZI’s launch needed to build trust across those touchpoints.

We developed a consistent identity for Turkish- and English-speaking audiences: visual language, typography, colour, composition and art direction. We connected the menu, culinary story, location, Google reviews and social content into a clear guest experience.

Before visiting, guests should understand the experience that awaits them.
Digital Clever’s approach
What we built

Building a brand around the guest experience.

01

Identity and original characters

We created characters inspired by signature dishes. Historical costumes, contemporary details and bold compositions express MAZI’s concept: palace cuisine reinterpreted for today.

02

03

Content that answers questions

The menu, interior, service and culinary story show the experience before booking.

04

Google as an acquisition channel

Our approach to review management helped make the restaurant’s Google profile a key source of new guests, particularly tourists.
