Health Solutions: making complex wellness services easier to understand

Health Solutions is a wellness project with locations in Bodrum and Ankara. Its communication needed to explain technologies and programmes in terms of people’s needs: recovery, body care, posture and wellbeing.

The client’s challenge

Make the centre’s offering clear before a consultation: who each service is for, how programmes differ and how to book. Different locations and audiences needed tailored messages, while potential partners needed a separate proposition.

What we did

01

Positioning and website copy

We prepared service and programme descriptions for Bodrum and Ankara, organising explanations around client needs and personalised programme selection.

02

Advertising propositions

We developed messaging for facial care, body shaping, recovery and posture programmes, connecting a specific need with the programme and the next step: consultation or booking.

03

Content and search

We prepared copy, headings and descriptions for the website and advertising materials, adapting the language for local audiences and different entry points.

04

Corporate partnership materials

We developed a proposal for Memorial Bodrum covering eligible groups, corporate benefits, an introductory experience day and launch steps. This is a prepared proposal, not a claim that a partnership was signed.

Project outcome

We prepared connected communication for service explanations, advertising, booking and discussions with a potential corporate partner. This case study presents the work and materials produced.
