BEAUTY / RETAIL / LA SULTANE DE SABA

A premium brand needs to communicate the value behind its price.

We adapted La Sultane de Saba’s French aesthetic for Turkish audiences, connecting the brand experience with sales.

The brand was little known locally. The product’s premium qualities were not yet clear in its communication.

We focused on making the brand desirable to try and give as a gift.

About the project

La Sultane de Saba is an internationally established French premium beauty brand, bringing its skincare rituals to the Turkish market.

The client’s challenge

Adapt an international beauty brand’s communication for Turkey and connect product discovery with purchase.

2025 · completed project

+68%sales growth in December
+47%sales growth in February
+126%growth in website conversion
+27%growth in average order value in the first month
Context

When a brand does not speak the market’s language, its value goes unheard.

Premium beauty · Launch and growth in Turkey

La Sultane de Saba entered Turkey with a strong heritage, a distinctive product and a recognisable French aesthetic. Local customers still needed to understand its premium price.

We preserved the ritual and premium positioning while clearly explaining the product, its audience, the experience and the reasons to return.

We made the brand easier to understand.
Digital Clever’s approach
What we built

Connecting the brand experience with purchase.

01
