MARKET ENTRY / OZBI / GOOD FOOD RUSSIA

Finding a familiar place for a new product in Turkey.

Bringing a Russian product to a new market: positioning, packaging, communication, buyers, influencers and first retail placements.

Russian sushki had no established category or familiar consumption occasion in Turkey.

The challenge was to give people a reason to try them.

About the project

OZBI is a Russian food brand associated with Good Food Russia / Diva Export. Familiar at home, the product had no established category or consumption occasion in Turkey.

The client’s challenge

Introduce the product to a new audience and prepare clear communication for market entry and retail discussions.

Context

In a new market, a product first needs to be understood.

Market entry · Food retail · Turkey

The product was unfamiliar to Turkish customers: the name carried no associations, the packaging did not explain the taste and there was no obvious place for it on the shelf.

We described the product as fragrant vanilla-flavoured mini bread rings, tested audience responses, prepared materials for Turkey and connected first impressions with retail buyer discussions.

Market entry begins with a question: “Why would someone here want this?”
Digital Clever’s approach
What we built

Creating a new point of entry.

01

Translating the meaning

We adapted the category and description so people could understand the product without knowledge of Russian culture.

02

Testing with people

We used focus groups to test taste, packaging and consumption occasions before scaling up.

03

Preparing for retail

We developed Turkish materials, messaging and arguments for buyer discussions.

04

Creating initial demand

Influencers, tastings and clear content gave people reasons to try the product.
