Why a good product does not sell in Turkey from day one

Entering a new market goes beyond translating the packaging. It takes a series of decisions that make a product understandable, desirable and accessible.

  • Define the local category before adapting the product description.
  • Test the language of benefits, visual identity and first contact.
  • Connect content, influencers and retail discussions into a coherent plan.

Start with the buying situation

Identify who chooses the product, when they choose it and what they compare it with. This shapes both the messaging and the first point of contact. Consumers and retail buyers may need different explanations of the same packaging.

Connect interest with the next step

Content should lead to ingredients, buying conditions or a supply discussion. Before increasing advertising, test the path yourself: are price, availability and contact details clear?
