423 SKUs: why SEO starts before the copy

With hundreds of products, the first task is to create a structure the catalogue can grow within, before writing individual descriptions.

  • Search demand: categories, specifications, applications and commercial intent.
  • Consistent rules for URLs, metadata, alt text and filter indexing.
  • Pages that help buyers compare products and contact the supplier.

Structure around the buyer’s needs

Buyers look for a solution defined by load capacity, material, diameter and mounting type. These specifications inform categories and filters. Not every filter needs its own indexable page.

Product pages should make comparison easier

Use consistent specifications, clear images and an easy way to enquire. Measure progress towards contact as well as visits, so SEO supports the commercial goal.
